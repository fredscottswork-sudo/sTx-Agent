Sport is Uganda's cheapest diplomacy

One good tournament does more for the country's reputation than a year of consultants.

BK
Brian Kaggwa
Sports Editor
25 October 2026, 09:00 3 min read Kampala

When the Cranes win, the country's name travels further than any trade delegation. When the athletes run, the flag appears in living rooms we could never buy into.

That is an extraordinary return on a modest investment, and yet sport remains the first line to be cut and the last to be planned.

Fund the facilities, pay the coaches, and let the federations run their competitions without interference. The rest follows.
